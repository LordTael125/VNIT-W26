2D Discrete Fourier Transform & Spectral Analysis

Pure NumPy Matrix Engine

Transform spatial image $f(x, y)$ to frequency domain $F(u, v)$ using separable matrix multiplications $F = W_M \cdot ((-1)^{x+y} f) \cdot W_N$.

Shifts zero-frequency DC component $F(0, 0)$ from corner $(0, 0)$ to frequency plane center $(M/2, N/2)$.

Default lab baseline is $D_0 = 15$ pixels radius from center.

Pattern Recognition & Invariant Spectral Feature Classifier

Invariant 32-D Feature Vector

Construct a compact invariant feature vector $[S_r; S_\theta] \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$ combining $R=16$ Concentric Radial Rings (rotation invariant) and $A=16$ Angular Wedges (scale invariant), normalized by total spectral energy (translation and illumination invariant).

Mathematical Invariance Properties:

1. Radial Ring Energies ($S_r$): $$S_r(k) = \sum_{r_k \le D(u, v) < r_{k+1}} |F(u, v)|^2$$ Rotating an image rotates its spectrum, preserving energy in each concentric ring (Rotation Invariance).
2. Angular Wedge Energies ($S_\theta$): $$S_\theta(m) = \sum_{\theta_m \le \theta < \theta_{m+1}} |F(u, v)|^2$$ Radial ray orientation is preserved under spatial scaling (Scale Invariance).
3. Normalization & Translation: $$\mathbf{v} = \frac{[S_r; S_\theta]}{\sum_{u, v} |F(u, v)|^2}$$ Spatial shifts only affect phase, leaving $|F(u, v)|$ identical (Translation Invariance).

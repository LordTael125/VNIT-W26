\midrule
\textbf{DDP-7. De-contaminable Floor} & Impermeable epoxy secondary containment slab + bolted skid anchors. & Civil EPC, MPCB, MIDC, Decomm. & Blocks heavy-metal soil seepage; cuts Year-15 closure cost by $>60\%$. \\
